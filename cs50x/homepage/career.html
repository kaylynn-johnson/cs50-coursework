In fall of 2018, I started college at the main campus for Texas A&M in College Station, TX. I majored in Aerospace Engineering with a minor in Mathematics.

In the summer of 2020, I interned at Lockheed Martin in Littleton, CO. I was in the Assembly, Integration and Test department for the Lucy mission. Throughout the summer, I was involved in generation of commands for system testing.

In the summer of 2021 and my senior year of college, I was an intern in the Mission Operations department at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory in Pasadena, CA. I initially starting working on the Sentinel-6A mission and moved to the SWOT mission by the time I graduated college. During my time as an intern, I worked on tool development for the mission operations team to help streamline operations.

In the spring of 2022, I graduated from Texas A&M with a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering. I graduated Summa Cum Laude with a 3.9/4 GPA.
