In fall of 2018, I started college at the main campus for Texas A&M in College Station, TX. I majored in Aerospace Engineering with a minor in Mathematics.

In the summer of 2020, I interned at Lockheed Martin in Littleton, CO. I was in the Assembly, Integration and Test department for the Lucy mission. Throughout the summer, I was involved in generation of commands for system testing.

In the summer of 2021 and my senior year of college, I was an intern in the Mission Operations department at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory in Pasadena, CA. I initially starting working on the Sentinel-6A mission and moved to the SWOT mission by the time I graduated college. During my time as an intern, I worked on tool development for the mission operations team to help streamline operations.

In the spring of 2022, I graduated from Texas A&M with a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering. I graduated Summa Cum Laude with a 3.9/4 GPA.

After graduation, I converted to full-time at NASA JPL with the same team I had interned with. I started as an operator for the Sentinel-6A mission, sending commands and analyzing telemetry. I was also on the mission development team for the SWOT mission that was to launch in December 2022.

On the Sentinel-6A and after launch for the SWOT mission, I was heavily involved with script generation to make operations easier for our very small team. Some of the scripts I worked on were command generation and validation and telemetry alarm notification.

Towards the end of my tenure at JPL, I also joined the mission development teams for CRISTAL and Sentinel-6B. For these missions, I worked on requirement generation and instrument operations manuals.

In the summer of 2024, I transition to a job at Peraton at the NASA White Sands Complex in Las Cruces, NM. This position was for an operations job for the STPSat-6 mission. I once again joined a very small team that was in charge of the satellite bus through the ground system. This provided me the opportunity to learn more about spacecraft and ground equipment. I utilized my software skills to again generate tools to streamline operations.
